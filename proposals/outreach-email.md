My name is [Your name] and I'm a data analyst student at Hyper Island. I've just finished my final individual project, and I wanted to share it with you because I think it could genuinely help [Company].

I built **swedish-el-oss** — a platform that tracks and forecasts Swedish electricity prices. It pulls Nord Pool spot prices, the real consumer bill (spot + tax + grid fee + VAT), and weather, hydro and gas drivers, then uses a machine-learning model to forecast prices for the next day, week and month (with a p10–p90 range). It's scoped to SE3/SE4 (Stockholm and the south) and refreshes daily.

**Why I think it fits you:** Stockholm electricity prices swing about **2x within a single day** — roughly 0.65 SEK/kWh overnight versus 1.40 at the evening peak. For a smart thermostat or a home battery, *when* you heat or charge is worth real money (studies show load-shifting can cut heating bills 8–46%). Acting on that needs good price and weather forecasts — and that's exactly the layer I've built. Rather than building it yourselves, you could plug this data and these forecasts straight into your product.

I've also made a **"warm your home for less" demo** (when to heat, cost, savings) and a ready **SE3/SE4 feature dataset**, which I'm happy to walk you through.

I know this is a student project, but I'd love to hear whether this data layer could help your work — would you be open to a short chat?
